# KADARI — Front-End & Conversion Authority

## Goal
The front end must turn the existing product into something people understand, experience, trust and buy.

Every major surface should answer one of five questions:
1. What is it?
2. Why should I care?
3. Show me.
4. Does this work for me?
5. What do I do next?

## Homepage hero
**Every asset. Accounted for.**

Supporting line:
Create living digital twins of the physical assets you own or manage and see your complete Asset Position in one place.

Primary CTA: **Try KADARI**
Secondary CTA: **See how it works**

Hero visual should show a real physical environment being scanned, with assets recognised and structured data emerging around them.

Do not lead with a generic dashboard screenshot.

## Homepage story sequence
1. You probably own or manage more than you think.
2. Assets accumulate and change over time.
3. Records become fragmented and stale.
4. KADARI turns the physical environment into living Digital Twins.
5. Those Digital Twins roll into one Asset Position.
6. KADARI surfaces value, evidence, history and gaps.
7. Show use cases.
8. Let the visitor try it.
9. Convert.

## Try It Now
This should be one of the strongest acquisition tools.

### Option A — Live camera
CTA: **Scan something near you**

Flow:
1. Open camera.
2. Scan one object.
3. Suggest identity / category / make / model where possible.
4. Show confidence and available fields.
5. Demonstrate evidence and value workflow.
6. Show Digital Twin completeness.
7. Explain: "This is the beginning of its KADARI Digital Twin."
8. CTA: **Build my Asset Position**

### Option B — Upload a photo
Desktop / restricted-camera fallback.

### Option C — Guided interactive demo
Use a real-feeling guided environment if anonymous live recognition is not production-ready.

Example environments:
- home / living room
- office
- workshop
- farm shed
- vehicle
- machinery

Do not use a video pretending to be interactive.

## Personal vs Business routing
Early in the journey ask:
- **I want to manage my own assets**
- **I want to manage assets for a business**
- **I manage assets for clients**

Same engine, different onboarding and copy.

## Personal onboarding
1. Create account
2. Choose starting category
3. Open camera
4. Scan first asset
5. Confirm
6. See first Digital Twin
7. See first Asset Position
8. Encourage second scan

## Business onboarding
1. Create organisation
2. Select business type
3. Add branches / locations
4. Select asset categories
5. Scan or import assets
6. See Asset Position
7. Surface evidence gaps
8. Invite unlimited team members when ready

## Professional onboarding
1. Create firm account
2. Create first client workspace
3. Create engagement
4. Define scope / locations / evidence requirements
5. Assign fieldwork
6. Capture / import assets
7. Review exceptions
8. Export engagement report / catalogue

Every use-case page should end in an appropriate CTA.

## Trust layer
Before users upload valuable asset information, answer clearly:
- Who can see this?
- Who owns the data?
- Can insurers or advisers access it automatically?
- What is private by default?
- What happens when I share something?
- How does KADARI distinguish declared vs verified information?

Principles:
- private by default
- permissioned sharing
- clear evidence status
- customer-controlled sharing
- auditable material changes

## Product proof
Show the product continuously through:
- camera capture
- recognition
- real asset records
- Asset Position dashboards
- confidence / evidence
- condition history
- valuation comparisons
- timelines
- shareable records

**Show the product before asking the customer to believe the product.**

## Conversion acceptance test
The front end is not commercially complete until a new visitor can answer:
- What is KADARI?
- What problem does it solve?
- How does the scan work?
- What is a Digital Twin?
- What is an Asset Position?
- Why does Value × Confidence matter?
- Does it work for me?
- Can I see it working?
- How much does it cost?
- Can I trust it with my information?
- What do I do next?
