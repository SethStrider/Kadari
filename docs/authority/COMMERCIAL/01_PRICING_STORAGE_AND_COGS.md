# KADARI — Pricing, Storage & COGS Authority

## Pricing principle
KADARI should not be priced like a lightweight home-inventory app.

The product includes Digital Twins, evidence, value, history, Asset Position, intelligence, storage and commercial integrations.

Pricing must preserve room for:
- 10–20% partner commission
- payment processing
- AI processing
- storage
- support
- third-party services where included

Do not position it as commodity file storage.

- Personal Plus: 50 GB

- Business Pro: 500 GB

## Unlimited users principle
All paid Business tiers should include **unlimited users** under the same company account.

KADARI gets more valuable when finance, operations, branch managers, stores, maintenance, custodians and management participate.

Do not use per-seat pricing as the primary business monetisation lever.

Price instead around:
- asset volume
- storage
- legal entities / client workspaces
- advanced integrations
- API usage
- third-party verification / data services
- enterprise support

## Branches vs legal entities
Branches / locations within the same company should not automatically require separate subscriptions.

Separate legal entities / complex groups may justify multi-entity pricing.

## COGS rule
The recurring subscription should comfortably fund normal infrastructure, standard AI usage and acquisition economics.

Expensive third-party services should be:
- included only in higher tiers,
- credit-based,
- or separately charged.

Do not include unlimited expensive verification inside low-priced subscriptions.

## Processing rule
Analyse once → persist structured result → refresh intelligently when required.

Do not re-run costly vision processing every time an asset record is opened.

## Professional pricing architecture
KADARI Professional should be priced by client workspace / engagement capacity, not seats.

Working structure:
- Professional Start: approx R1,499/month / $99/month, limited client workspaces
- Professional: approx R2,999/month / $199/month, larger client estate
- Professional Scale: custom
- additional client workspace: separately priced where appropriate

Exact Professional limits remain to be modelled before final launch.

## Partner economics
Referral / distribution commissions may vary between 10% and 20% depending on channel and product.

Pricing must be designed with this economics baked in from the start.
