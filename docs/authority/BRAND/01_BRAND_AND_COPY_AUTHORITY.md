# KADARI — Brand & Copy Authority

## Brand
**KADARI**

## Mathematical signature
**Σ(V × C)**

## Primary line
**Every asset. Accounted for.**

## Supporting lines
- Know what you have. Know what it’s worth. Keep the proof.
- Walk. Scan. Confirm.
- The evidence should exist before you need it.
- One physical asset. One living digital record.
- Assets change. Their record should change with them.
- Value is only as useful as the confidence behind it.
- Your accounting system knows the number. KADARI knows the asset behind it.

## Positioning
KADARI is the persistent digital identity, evidence and intelligence layer for physical assets.

It is not positioned as:
- an insurer
- a home inventory app
- an accounting package
- generic fixed asset software
- a crypto / tokenisation platform
- a cloud photo vault

## Brand personality
KADARI should feel:
- intelligent
- calm
- premium
- modern
- credible
- globally usable
- human enough for individuals
- serious enough for enterprise

## Visual direction
Use:
- graphite / near-black foundations
- vivid teal / emerald accents
- mint highlights
- clean white / light surfaces
- strong modern sans-serif typography
- real-world asset photography
- camera / scan / recognition visual language
- structured overlays and data that emerge around the physical object
- physical object + digital counterpart relationship

Avoid:
- insurer shields as the dominant visual
- gold wealth-management aesthetics
- generic SaaS gradients
- crypto visual language
- sci-fi hologram clichés
- sterile accounting imagery
- random AI iconography

## Visual principle
**The physical asset remains the hero. Technology surrounds it.**

The experience should visually communicate:
**physical → scanned → identified → evidenced → valued → accounted for**

## Formula use
Σ(V × C) is not decorative mathematics. It represents value multiplied by confidence across the asset estate.

Use it selectively in:
- brand board
- hero treatments
- reports
- dashboards
- presentations
- processing / intelligence states
- physical asset tag concepts

Do not force the equation into every small logo execution.

## Copy principles
Write simply and specifically.

Avoid:
- revolutionary
- cutting-edge
- AI-powered as an empty claim
- blockchain-style language
- technical digital-twin jargon in the hero
- fear-based insurance copy

The technology may be sophisticated. The explanation must remain simple.

## Trustli relationship
KADARI is a standalone brand. Do not use **KADARI by Trustli** as the master brand.

Trustli may be referenced where it genuinely provides identity, verification, consent or evidentiary infrastructure.
